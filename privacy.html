Privacy Policy

Cosmic Bounce · Effective November 1, 2026

Cosmic Bounce is made by Stephen Stuthers ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: I don't run any servers, I don't ask you to make an account, and I never see your personal information. The game uses services from Apple and Google, described below.

Your game progress

Your progress, medals, stamps and settings are saved on your iPhone. If you're signed into iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account so it survives reinstalling the app and follows you to other devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; I can't see it.

Game Center

If you sign into Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple's Game Center. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy, and your Game Center nickname may be visible to other players on the leaderboards. I don't receive your Apple Account details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and basic app and device information (such as iOS version and crash diagnostics).

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you say no, you still get ads, but they won't be personalized using your IDFA. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Controls & Guide → Privacy Choices.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): showing personalized ads may count as "sharing" personal information. You can opt out under Controls & Guide → Privacy Choices, or by turning off tracking as above.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your payment details. The game only checks with Apple whether you own the purchase.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell your personal information.
  • I don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game has no chat and no player accounts of its own.

Children

Cosmic Bounce is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to family-friendly content ratings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
