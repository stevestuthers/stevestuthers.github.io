Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
No, as long as you're signed into iCloud. Your progress is saved to your own iCloud account and comes back when you reinstall.

I bought Remove Ads on another device.
Open Controls & Guide and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Open Controls & Guide and tap Privacy Choices (shown where it applies). You can also turn off app tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
