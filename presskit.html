Short description

One astronaut, nine planets and nine monsters. Cosmic Bounce is a fast arcade climber you can pick up in seconds: bounce up hand-built worlds from Mercury to Pluto, beat the monster that guards each one, then keep climbing forever in Deep Space and take on a new Daily Challenge every day.

About the game

Every planet is a hand-built climb with its own twist: Mercury's rock crumbles in the sun, Venus has acid clouds, Earth has a rocket to catch before lift-off, Mars has dust devils, Jupiter has storm winds and time dilation, Saturn has ring conveyors, Uranus flips gravity, Neptune has black ice and Pluto is dark.

A monster guards the way out of every planet, each with one attack to learn and a weak spot to find. Land five hits for the knockout. After Pluto, Deep Space goes on forever, with new galaxies, portal runs and comets.

The Daily Challenge gives every player the same fresh climb each day. Stars are your oxygen, the tempo rises act by act, and twelve portal mini-games break up the climb. Keep your streak and see where you rank on Game Center.

Features

  • 9 hand-built planets and 9 monster fights
  • Endless Deep Space after the campaign
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, the same for every player, with 12 portal mini-games
  • Steer by pressing either side of the screen, or by tilting the device
  • 7 lifesavers to bank and fire: jetpack, moon boots, safety net, force field, grapple hook, chrono watch and supernova
  • 36 Game Center achievements and leaderboards
  • Plays offline; progress follows you through iCloud

About the developer

Cosmic Bounce is the first game from Stuthers Games, made by one developer and shaped by more than 50 TestFlight builds of player feedback.
